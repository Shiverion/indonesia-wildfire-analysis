# Globe assets and attribution

- Earth surface texture: NASA Blue Marble, *Land Surface, Ocean Color and Sea Ice* (NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / Visible Earth). The synchronized manifest records the exact source URL and SHA-256.
- Boundaries: geoBoundaries `IDN-ADM1-65028918`, source OpenStreetMap / Wambacher; © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The generated legacy Kalimantan Timur display feature is a topological union of current East and North Kalimantan, not a historical-boundary claim.

See `/geo/manifest.json` for frozen source URLs, hashes, and the derived-geometry record.
