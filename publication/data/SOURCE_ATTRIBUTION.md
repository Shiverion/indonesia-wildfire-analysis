# Source attribution for the Phase 3 analysis bundle

This coordinate-free derived dataset was produced from:

- NASA VIIRS VNP14A1.002 and VNP14IMG.002 active-fire products, with NASA Terra MOD14A1.061 and Aqua MYD14A1.061 used only for registered history-gap sensitivity support;
- NASA MOD13Q1.061 Enhanced Vegetation Index;
- Climate Hazards Center CHIRPS daily precipitation (Funk et al., 2015, https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2015.66);
- Copernicus Climate Change Service ERA5-Land (Muñoz-Sabater et al., 2021, https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-13-4349-2021);
- the global peatland extent ensemble archived at Zenodo record 19731872; and
- MapBiomas Indonesia Collection 4.1 annual land cover and land use, version 4.1.1 (official ATBD: https://landy.mapbiomas.id/assets/files/ATBD%20Mapbiomas%20ID%20Col%204.1%20EN.pdf).

The derived files contain pseudonymous identifiers and no coordinates. They do not imply endorsement by any provider. See `DATA_LICENSE.md` for provider-specific terms, limitations, and redistribution boundaries.
